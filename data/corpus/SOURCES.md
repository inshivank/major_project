## Notes

- These summaries are for retrieval and demonstration only, not for clinical use.
- Before publishing or expanding the corpus, verify each source's terms of use and
  record the licence here.
- Emergency numbers are jurisdiction-specific. The system must direct users to their
  local emergency number rather than assume one country.
